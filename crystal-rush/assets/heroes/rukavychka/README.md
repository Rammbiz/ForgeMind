# rukavychka art (assets/heroes/rukavychka) — champion C29 «Рукавичка — Мишка-шкряботушка» (wave 9, heroes_design.md §6.31)

- `card.png` — the 3:4 card (Gemini, 2026-10-10; 1792x2400 source in `art_src/heroes/rukavychka/rukavychka_card_src.jpg`), scaled to
  1152x1543, cut out (tools/art/cutlib.py).
  After the Ukrainian folk tale «Рукавичка» (our own drawing, not the Jan Brett or Yaroslava Mills illustrations): a grey
  mouse-girl in a red dotted headscarf, a white embroidered blouse, a cream sheepskin vest and a long dark-red skirt, waving;
  on her back a huge knitted cream-and-red mitten-house with a shuttered window and a little chimney, a hare, a frog and a
  fox peeking out of its cuff; a round faceted clear quartz in a silver bezel on her chest (the heart gem; the in-game
  guest counter sits on this medallion).
- `splash.png` — the same art (cameos and walkouts use the splash slot).
- Framing: `HeroArt.META["rukavychka"]` (eyes at x 0.70, y 0.34 of the card).
