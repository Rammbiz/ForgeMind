# franko art (assets/heroes/franko) — champion C28 «Франко — Каменяр» (wave 9, heroes_design.md §6.30)

- `card.png` — the 3:4 card (Gemini, 2026-10-10; 1792x2400 source in `art_src/heroes/franko/franko_card_src.jpg`), scaled to
  1152x1543, cut out (tools/art/cutlib.py).
  A respectful heroic portrait inspired by Ivan Franko and his poem «Каменярі» (not the banknote portrait): a broad-shouldered
  man with a reddish moustache and short beard, a white Galician shirt with red-and-black embroidery under a quarryman's
  leather harness and apron, a hammer cut from a granite block with two brass bands and a cushion-cut golden topaz set into
  the stone (the heart gem), raised over the right shoulder; broken iron shackles on both wrists (always broken).
