# UI direction — owner decisions (binding)

Direction: **Genshin Impact × AFK Journey fusion** (owner: "Genshin looks very beautiful, AFK Journey too — combine them").
AFK Journey is a PORTRAIT game (owner correction) — use it as the direct layout reference for our 720x1280 portrait screens;
Genshin (landscape) is adapted.

Round 1 answers:
- From Genshin: light cream panels with gold lines; the character screen (3D hero on a soft element-coloured stage, attribute
  tabs, constellation/talents); rarity cards with gradient rarity backgrounds + clean element icons and star marks.
  (NOT chosen: the wish ceremony — keep our own Portal ceremony design.)
- From AFK Journey: painted hero portraits (big warm painterly art on cards, hero screens, summons); storybook look and frames
  (parchment, painted frames and ornaments); warm fairy-tale palette (soft sunlight, cosy colours).
  (NOT chosen: world-as-menu map.)
- World vs UI: "The dark cosmic world is only one of the levels." Mix approach is OK but MUST BE BRIGHTER: menus light and warm;
  the run HUD also light (no heavy dark glass); worlds vary in mood, space is just one world.
- Home screen: the hero in a live 3D scene (large hero on a pedestal at the start of the bridge in warm light, machines nearby,
  minimal buttons, a big precious "Play").

Round 2 answers:
- Typography: EVERYTHING in one soft, rounded, friendly font like Genshin's (titles just heavier). No storybook serif. (Find a Cyrillic-capable
  Genshin-like rounded humanist face; verify і ї є ґ ’.)
- Navigation: bottom bar like AFK Journey (light bar, painted icons with labels, the active tab rises).
- Buttons: cream buttons with a thin gold edge (Genshin) for ordinary actions; key actions (Грати, Призвати, Покращити) are warm
  gold-amber with a crystal, richly rendered (bitmap).
- Hero collection card: painted portrait from the splash (AFK) on a gradient background in the gem colour (Genshin), thin gold frame,
  gem emblem top, class + element, name and level at the bottom.

Round 3 answers:
- Hero screen (portrait): opens on the full-screen painted splash (gem emblem, name, title, badges, skills row — like the owner's
  SR/UR references); a "Деталі" button switches to the live 3D hero at the top on a gem-coloured stage + a cream bottom sheet with
  tabs (Атрибути / Навички / Спорядження / Зірки), Genshin-style.
- Icons: mix — clean line icons in thin rings for menus, classes, elements (Genshin); painted colour icons for items, currencies,
  skills and the bottom bar (AFK).
- Run HUD: light cream plates with a soft shadow and gold edge; ult button = painted portrait in a gold ring. Readable on any world.
- Motion: menus soft and smooth (Genshin: gentle fades, soft glow); rewards, summons and upgrades juicy (flashes, shake, sound).

Round 4 (after home concept v1, concepts/home_v1.jpg):
- Style: "MUCH closer to Genshin" — remove the casual-cartoon look (thick text outlines, chibi heroine, emoji-like icons). Refined,
  restrained, elegant Genshin-grade UI: thin gold hairlines, soft text without heavy strokes, delicate ornament, translucent layering,
  anime-influenced refined art; keep the light/warm palette, the composition and component set of home_v1 (top bar, ribbon, edge
  buttons, gem PLAY button, raised-medallion bottom nav), our gem/facet signature.
- Heroes in 3D: REALISTIC proportions like the painted splash (no chibi).
